# 🚀 Introducing Smart Complaint Prioritizer

**From complaints to insights, from insights to preventive action.**

I'm excited to share **Smart Complaint Prioritizer**, an ideathon project concept focused on making campus complaint management smarter and more organized.

💡 **Our idea:** Prioritize campus complaints based on urgency, safety risks, the number of people affected, recurring issues, and location criticality.

### ✨ Key Features

* 🚨 Explainable complaint prioritization
* 🛡️ Safety and urgency-based scoring
* 👥 Assessment of people affected
* 🔍 Identification of emerging risk patterns
* 📊 Complaint tracking and status monitoring

🎯 **Our goal:** Help campus teams identify important issues sooner and use recurring complaint patterns to support better maintenance decisions.

This is an early-stage student project concept, and I'm looking forward to developing it further with my team.
